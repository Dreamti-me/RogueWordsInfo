Privacy Policy for RogueWords

Effective date: September 23, 2026

RogueWords is developed and published by Dreamtime LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play RogueWords and use its online features.

Game Accounts

RogueWords uses Microsoft PlayFab for online game services. When the game starts, it signs in to a guest game account using a device-derived identifier: on Android, the Android ID; on iOS, Apple's identifier for vendor, which is specific to apps from Dreamtime on your device. The account is created automatically. You do not need to provide a name, email address, or password, and RogueWords does not ask for them.

Gameplay and Online Features

Game progress, saved games, and settings are stored on your device. Online features send relevant information to PlayFab, including your game account identifier, token balance and spending, daily bonus claims and their times, reward grants, and early-access entitlements. We use this information to keep your tokens and entitlements in sync, deliver bonuses and rewards, and prevent duplicate grants or abuse.

RogueWords downloads its word database from our servers on Microsoft Azure. To do this, the game asks PlayFab for a download link and then downloads the file. These requests include your game account identifier and are logged by our service providers.

RogueWords does not currently show ads, offer in-app purchases, or publish online leaderboards. If we add any of these features, we will update this policy before they are released.

Service Providers and Information Sharing

We share the information needed to operate these features with Microsoft PlayFab and Azure, which process it on our behalf. These providers may process information on servers outside your country. Their handling of information is also described in Microsoft's Privacy Statement. If you download RogueWords from Google Play or the Apple App Store, those stores also process information under their own policies. We do not sell your information.

RogueWords does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. Service providers do receive network information, such as your IP address, and may estimate a general location from it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain game-account, token, bonus, and reward records as needed to operate your account, maintain entitlements, prevent fraud and duplicate rewards, and meet applicable legal obligations. Microsoft also applies its own retention practices.

You can request deletion of your RogueWords game account and associated data by emailing support@dreamti.me. Include enough information to help us locate the account, such as your device model and an approximate date of play. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or payment-card details. We may ask for information to verify that the account belongs to you, and we will explain any records that must be retained and why. Removing the app clears local data subject to your device's backup settings, but does not itself delete server-side account records.

Children's Privacy

RogueWords is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The device identifier described above is used only to operate the game and its online features. If you believe a child has provided personal information through RogueWords, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Security

RogueWords uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections for its online services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as RogueWords changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
