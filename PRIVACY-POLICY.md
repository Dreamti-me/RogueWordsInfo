# Privacy Policy for Lexicriss (formerly RogueWords)

RogueWords is now called Lexicriss. Its privacy policy, effective November 14, 2026, is at:

- https://dreamti.me/lexicriss/privacy.html (the page the game links to)
- [privacy-policy.html](privacy-policy.html) on this site (the same text)

In short: nothing is sent to our game server until you choose **Play online**, and you can turn online play off at any time under **Privacy** on the main menu. Every ad is non-personalized, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

Questions and data-deletion requests: **support@dreamti.me** (Dreamtime LLC, Washington, USA).
